**Senior iOS Developer** · mobile banking & fintech

I build mobile banking apps: from architecture and the first screen to release. Most of that work happens in long-lived codebases, where the point is to make things clearer and safer without breaking what already works for customers.

## Focus

- **Mobile banking** — payments, transfers, cards, deposits, loans, support chat.
- **Legacy code** — first I understand it, then I change it in small steps.
- **End to end** — product discussion, estimates, code, review, build, release.

## Background

I've spent my career in mobile, most of it in fintech: retail and business banking apps, both built from scratch and inherited with more history than documentation. I know what it takes to design architecture for a new product and to keep an old one moving without breaking what customers rely on.

I own a feature end to end: discuss it with product and design, propose a solution, estimate, write the code, review the team's, prepare the build and ship it. I have mentored juniors, run releases and worked directly with clients, backend and design teams.
